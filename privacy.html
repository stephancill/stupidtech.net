Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Overview

Stupid Technology builds small, focused apps. We keep data collection to a minimum, and where an app works entirely on your device we do not collect your data at all.

Data we collect

Most Stupid Technology apps are local-first. For example, the stupid totp authenticator stores your codes and secrets on your device only and does not transmit them anywhere.

Some apps connect to servers we operate (such as evm.stupidtech.net and search.stupidtech.net) only to fulfil the request you make. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
